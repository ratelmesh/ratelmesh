RatelMesh v0.2.36

This release finishes the multilingual public product surface and corrects the
macOS menu-bar brand presentation.

Highlights

- English is authoritative at the unprefixed website routes, including
  `/security`.
- Complete public pages for English, Spanish, German, French, Japanese, Korean,
  Italian, Dutch, Polish, Swedish, Brazilian Portuguese, Simplified Chinese and
  Traditional Chinese.
- Canonical URLs, permanent locale aliases, `hreflang`, OpenGraph locales and
  the sitemap cover all 13 languages.
- The macOS menu uses a native template mark without the former white halo or
  oversized raster treatment, while retaining a VoiceOver label.
- The website now uses the accepted honey-badger Mesh identity in its social
  preview and responsive navigation.
- Cross-language security, privacy and fail-closed claims retain their original
  technical meaning.
- Independently reviewed across multiple implementations, followed by
  integration fixes and full Mac mini release gates.

Artifact status

- macOS: Developer ID Application and Installer signed; Apple notarization
  submitted and currently pending.
- Windows: beta ZIPs for x64 and ARM64; not Authenticode signed.
- Linux: beta archives for x64 and ARM64.
- Android: debug-signed test APK; not a Play Store release.
- iOS: unsigned developer build; not directly installable and not a TestFlight
  release.

Install only from https://ratelmesh.com or https://download.ratelmesh.com.
Verify downloads with SHA256SUMS.txt.
